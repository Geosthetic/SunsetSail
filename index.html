Frequently Asked Questions

Can I book just the cruise and add flights later?

Yes. We can lock in a cruise cabin now and add air, hotel, and transfers whenever your plans are firm — though bundling early typically saves 10–20% versus booking separately.

Do you handle flights from smaller cities?

Yes. We book from most major U.S. and Canadian airports as well as many regional ones, and we can arrange overnight connections to guarantee you arrive a day early.

What happens if my flight is delayed and I miss the ship?

Because we bundle air with the cruise line, protected-airfare programs can rebook you to the next port at little or no cost, subject to the program's terms. We also recommend arriving a day early — and we price that hotel night into your bundle.

How far in advance should I book?

For cruises, 6–12 months ahead gets the best cabin selection and group rates. Caribbean and Alaska peak-season sailings often sell out of bundled air allocations 4–6 months out.

Why does the price change when I pick my airport?

Airfare is the largest variable in any bundle. Where you fly from, how flexible your dates are, and whether the cruise line's air program covers your gateway all shift the total. The price you see is a live estimate built from your selections; your written offer confirms it.
