Frequently Asked Questions

Can I book just the cruise and add flights later?

Yes — though bundling early typically saves 10–20% versus booking separately.

Do you handle flights from smaller cities?

Yes, from virtually any U.S. or Canadian airport, including overnight connections so you arrive a day early.

What if my flight is delayed and I miss the ship?

Bundled "protected airfare" programs can rebook you to the next port at little or no cost.

How far in advance should I book?

6–12 months for best cabin selection; peak-season bundled air often allocates out 4–6 months ahead.
